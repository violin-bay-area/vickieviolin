About

Rooted in the Russian
violin tradition

Victoria Yakubovich is a professional violinist and dedicated educator with over 30 years of teaching experience. A graduate of the Gnesin State Music College in Moscow, she brings world-class conservatory training to the Bay Area.

With an extensive background in orchestral performance, chamber music, and higher education, Victoria combines rigorous technique with a warm, encouraging teaching style suited to each individual student.

Her students regularly perform in youth orchestras and chamber ensembles, and are successfully prepared for competitions, auditions, and graded exams.
