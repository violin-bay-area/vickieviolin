Masterclass

1:1 masterclass
sessions

A private, one-on-one intensive session with Victoria focused entirely on your technique, interpretation, and performance. Ideal for students who want a deep dive beyond their regular weekly lesson.

Book a Masterclass
Format

What the session covers

  • ♩
    One-on-one technique work

    Victoria works with you directly on bowing technique, left-hand position, shifting, vibrato, intonation, or any area you want to improve — no group dynamic, full focus on you.

  • ♩
    Repertoire coaching

    Bring a piece you are working on — a concerto, sonata, étude, or competition piece. Victoria will help you unlock its musical depth and refine your interpretation.

  • ♩
    Performance preparation

    Preparing for an audition, exam, or recital? Masterclass sessions are perfect for targeted, high-intensity performance prep with honest, constructive feedback.

  • ♩
    Flexible focus

    You set the agenda. Whether it is sight-reading, scale work, bow distribution, tone production, or a specific passage — the session is built around your needs.

Who it's for

  • Intermediate and advanced students
  • Students preparing for competitions or auditions
  • Pre-conservatory and college-level players
  • Students who want an intensive technique deep-dive
  • Adult players returning to the instrument

What to bring

  • Your violin, bow, and rosin
  • A prepared piece or specific passage
  • Your sheet music
  • A list of technique areas you'd like to focus on
