About our recitals

A celebration, not a competition

Recitals at Victoria's studio are designed to be encouraging and fun. Every student performs — regardless of level — and the audience is always made up of supportive families and friends.

Performing in front of others is an important part of musical development. It builds confidence, teaches students to recover gracefully from small mistakes, and gives real meaning to all the practice time at home.

New students typically perform in their first recital within 6–9 months of starting lessons, once they have a piece they feel proud of.

When

Spring (May/June) and Winter (December) each year. Families are notified 6–8 weeks in advance.

Where

Held at a local venue in the Bay Area. Details are shared with enrolled students.

Who performs

All enrolled students are invited. Participation is strongly encouraged but never mandatory.
